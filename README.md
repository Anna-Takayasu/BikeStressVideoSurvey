# Video Comparison Survey — Participant Instructions

Thank you for taking part in this study.

## Before you start

- Please use a **desktop or laptop computer**.
- Please use a stable internet connection.
- Please complete the survey in a normal browser window (not Private/Incognito mode).
- If you stop and continue later, please use the **same device, same browser, and the same survey URL**.

## Survey link

Open:
[
https://Anna-Takayasu.github.io/BikeStressVideoSurvey/](https://anna-takayasu.github.io/BikeStressVideoSurvey/)

## What to do

1. Click **Start / Resume**.
2. Complete the short sociodemographic questionnaire.
3. For each comparison, click **Play video sequence**.
4. The **Top video** plays first, followed automatically by the **Bottom video**.
5. Please watch both videos completely.
6. Answer the question:

   **Which video looks more stressful / unsafe?**

   Choose:
   - **Top video**
   - **Same**
   - **Bottom video**

7. Continue until the survey is finished.

## If you need to stop before finishing

Click **Save partial result**.

Microsoft Forms will open with your current result already filled in.  
Please click **Submit** in Microsoft Forms.

To continue later, reopen the **same survey URL** using the **same browser and device**, then click **Start / Resume**.

## When you finish

At the end of the survey:

1. Click **Submit result in Microsoft Forms**.
2. Microsoft Forms will open with your result already filled in.
3. Please click **Submit** in Microsoft Forms.

After Microsoft Forms confirms your submission, the survey is complete.

Thank you very much for your participation.
